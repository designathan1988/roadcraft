Symptom: while navigating a large city the frame rate drops in bursts (the "lento ao navegar" the player reports); a still view holds 60 fps. The shadow pass counts of a street pan (large city seed 7, probe build, 2026-10-22) showed cascades 0 and 1 drawn whole in 121 of 121 frames (`why.noStore` 236): the whole town's near casters into two shadow maps every frame of a pan.

Trace:
- src/render/renderer.ts:586-620 (before) `casterSignature` hashed every still caster into ONE number; src/render/renderer.ts:3540 (before) `still = signature === last && !castersStirred`.
- src/render/sunCascades.ts pass(): `if (!still) storeValid[i] = false` threw every cascade's store away whenever the number changed.
- The number changes at nearly every frame of a pan: src/render/buildings/partCells.ts `choose`/`levelAt` swap part runs' levels by pixels as the eye moves (the run's geometry id changes), src/render/cellInstances.ts:872-920 `cull` + `reconcile` rewrite the props', gardens' and furniture's instance buffers (their versions), src/render/buildings/shellBatch.ts `setVisible`/`add`/`remove` bump `castVersion` as cells come and go, src/render/signals.ts and src/render/poleLayer.ts write instance matrices as blocks come into view.
- src/render/renderer.ts:446 (before) `onAssetsReady = () => { stir(); askDraw(); }`: every arrival (a cell's meshes, a program, a worker's answer) also threw the stores away (`castersStirred`), 20 call sites.
- src/render/sunCascades.ts:613 `all = ... || (moved && !storeValid[i])`: with the wind's sway casters moving every frame (renderer.ts `swaying.moving: () => true`), a store thrown away means the cascade is drawn whole that same frame.
- src/render/sunCascades.ts:37 and :457-460 (before): the sun held in 0.1-degree steps for all cascades at once (the city's clock 20x real time, src/sim/city/city.ts:6) refit and redrew the three cascades whole in one frame every 1.5-2 s of play; a pan past the square's room (20-43 % of the half-side) refit and redrew a cascade whole though its texels were unchanged.

Root cause: the shadow stores' validity was one bit for the whole town, so any change anywhere (a level swap, a prop culled at the view's edge, a texture landing) threw every cascade's store away and redrew the whole town into the near cascades; a pan produces such changes every frame.

Fix:
1. src/render/renderer.ts `casterChanges`: a signature per caster (what it draws with: geometry, materials and their maps, layers, buffer versions, place, count) and the world boxes of what changed, kept apart from each other (src/render/changeBoxes.ts): a batch says what changed in it (`userData.castBox`, shellBatch.ts, farParts.ts), a layer's cells say which instances came or went (`userData.castBoxes`, cellInstances.ts reconcile), an instanced mesh with its matrices on the page is diffed instance by instance (signals, poles: poleCells.ts keeps the matrices), a plain mesh by the box round its geometry's sphere in the world. `onAssetsReady` no longer throws the stores away.
2. src/render/sunCascades.ts: `ShadowPassInput.patch` carries those boxes; each cascade clears and draws again only the strips its changed casters fall in (at most `MOST_STRIPS`, under `SCROLL_MOST` of the map; the shadow camera narrowed to the strip, the shadow's viewport on it, the full matrix put back), keeps its store, and draws the movers over as after a whole draw. A square that only moved by whole texels scrolls its store (blit) and draws the strips uncovered. Each cascade holds its own sun until a texel of shadow would move; refits alone are drawn one cascade a frame.
3. src/render/buildings/gpuBatchedMesh.ts: `compact` rewrites the index buffer from each geometry's own indices kept on the page (two bytes each), no `getBufferSubData` readback on the page.

Verified (probe build, large city seed 7, 1920x1080 ultra, shadow stats of a 2 s street pan): cascades 0/1 whole draws 121/121 -> 12/5 (run 10), patches 269; district pan 130/130 -> 18/18, patches 243. Photos of map, district and street unchanged to the eye (no strips, no holes).
