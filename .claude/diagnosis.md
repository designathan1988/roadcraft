Fix:
- world/carriageway onRoadGround: the point within the footway's half width of a road's whole centre line (tunnels left out, as touchesRoad does), or inside a junction's footway ring; the same one cell read (someAt), a junction's ring flattened once (identityMemo). natureOpen and coverFree ask it; the trees the player plants (plantedKeeps) and the other callers keep onCarriageway.
- riggedCitizens finish: each face row's range given as row * 4 components from i * row * 4 (three's componentStride): three then sends texels (0, i) to (row - 1, i), exactly that figure's row.
- riggedCitizens keepAll: every body of the roster (16) asked for when the renderer is made (src/render/renderer.ts, agents.keepPeople) and held: never abandoned, never dropped from the queue, never evicted. The cast people are drawn the first frame a car is near. The cost is the 16 bodies resident (their geometry arrays already go to the GPU only, keepPartOnGpu).
- Facades and masses: the pulse filtered by a tent (triangle of radius w, Inigo Quilez "filterable procedurals": the second integral of the pulse), evaluated on fract(x) so floats keep their precision, the fade to the mean from 2 pixels a bay to 1 (smoothstep(0.5, 1.0, w)), the footprint the gradient's length per axis instead of fwidth. The tent's response is sinc squared (first side lobe -26.5 dB against the box's -13 dB): the windows stay sharp down to 2 pixels without the shimmer. No pass, target or texture added: a few ALU operations per facade pixel.
