Engine Geometry Catalogue

Geometry Optimization

Every object of the catalogue measured against a triangle budget for its kind, with what to change in it (each object's card lists its own changes). Nothing in the game was changed.

What goes into the game, and what is loaded for nothing

  • Roads: three versions, all in the game. Near the camera each block of road is drawn at full detail (the "Roads" rows, their L0); farther, the same block's far level, simplified by the road workers (coarseArrays, their L1); and wherever the far budget let a block's far level go, or while a large city opens, the network's far level made from the roads' outlines alone ("Roads: network far level"). The near level is the costly one: 2,040 to 12,228 triangles for 140 m of road, against a strip of a few hundred and the markings in the shader.
  • People: the MakeHuman pipeline ships with the game. When a body has no cooked file, riggedCitizens.ts builds it at run time (personBuild.ts: the base mesh and its shape targets, 17.6 MB, by loadPeopleAssets, and every garment and hair it wears by loadProxyItem); the procedural crowd loads brows, lashes and other pieces the same way (proceduralCrowd.ts), and their textures (skinAppearance.ts). The game should load one baked skinned mesh a body (clothes and hair in it, one texture atlas) and nothing of MakeHuman.
  • What the code loads into the game, read file by file:
    • public/models/people is 313 MB, 2,391 files. scripts/vite/model-urls-plugin.ts emits every file of the folder into the build because people/body/proxy.ts (imported by the game's page and its people worker) imports virtual:model-urls/people/proxies; the last build (dist/assets) carries 511 .bin, 510 .json and 1,000 .webp of it (258 MB of .bin alone).
    • Of the 575 MakeHuman items, the game can put only 133 on anybody (30.7 MB): the system outfits, shoes, hair, brows, lashes, hats and eyes of spec.ts WARDROBE, two community outfits of roster.ts, and the street-selected dresses, suits, glasses, jewelry, beards, shoes and hair randomPerson picks from. The other 442 (266.3 MB: weapons, horns, masks, superhero suits, bikinis, the 155,930-triangle bikini, the 91,704-triangle sandals...) are never worn by the game and still shipped. List: people-reach.json. The people saved by the Person Creator are kept in the city file (world/doc.ts people) but nothing in src/render draws them.
    • Every cast body is read from cooked/people/mh_*.bin (16 bodies, 43 MB, 3 to 3.9 MB each), but riggedCitizens.ts personAsset then calls loadSkinAppearance, which calls loadProxyItem for each garment, hair, brow, lash and beard the body wears: the item's .json and whole .bin are fetched and its texture decoded and read back on the CPU only to find its texture file. When the cooked file is stale or missing (its fingerprint, cookedLoad.ts), every body is built from MakeHuman during play (personBuild.ts: the base mesh and 17.6 MB of shape targets, every item fitted).
    • The procedural crowd (proceduralCrowd.ts) always calls env.setup() (proceduralBake.ts: loadPeopleAssets, the base mesh and its shape targets, and a Morpher) when a class is built and for every person added, loads the eyes proxy, and fetches and fits at run time the casual suits, shoes, eyebrows, eyelashes and stock hair each class wears (wornItem, cls.fit). The 8 classes are cooked (cooked/procedural, 10.4-10.5 MB each, 101 MB with the 29 hairstyles), the garments are not.
    • The Person Creator is a separate page (people-lab.html, src/peopleLab): it is the only one that needs the MakeHuman base, shape targets, face units, skins and items. The game's build has only index.html as its entry, yet takes them all through people/body/* and render/people/*.
    • What the game should load (as GTA does): one finished skinned mesh a person or class, clothes, hair and face already in it, one texture atlas, its levels of detail; no base mesh, no shape targets, no proxies, no fitting at run time. The MakeHuman pipeline stays in the Person Creator and in the cook that writes those meshes.
    • public/models/citizens holds only two licence texts; CITIZEN_ASSET_URLS (citizenAssets.ts) is empty, so the GLTFLoader branch in riggedCitizens.ts load() is dead code. public/models/elements: all 22 glTF are used (elements.ts MODELS) with 6 PNG textures (Leaves 2.6 MB, Rocks 2.5 MB, uncompressed).
  • Why a flat road is so dense (code): roadSurfaceReads.ts makes each band of a road a surface of its own (verge, footway, dropped kerb, kerb, asphalt, median kerb, planting, paving, and one more for each paint colour), each with its own skirt, and mesh/tileShape.ts refine() bisects every triangle until no edge is longer than maxEdge: half a terrain cell (3.2 m) on the ground, 6.4 m raised or on flat land (roadSurfacePass.ts), 0.75 m on dropped kerbs, 5 for paint. A local road of 140 m is 16 such surfaces, 2,598 triangles. The bands and the markings should be one strip with the kerb, joints and lines in the shader, vertices only where the road bends or the ground changes.
  • The cast's bodies: 28,736 to 45,256 triangles near, 84 bytes a vertex; a city-builder figure a few pixels tall needs a few thousand at most near, a few hundred beyond.
  • Buildings' far facade (L1) keeps almost all of the near level (apartments 11,156 against 12,258): its balconies, railings, units and pots stay as geometry, and 90,284 hidden triangles over the 80 buildings.
  • What the pictures show, object by object: building walls cut into many rectangles round every opening (one quad a face is enough, openings in the shader); storey bands and stains under sills built as triangles; roof drums of 14 sides; lot foundations and paved lots as grids of strips and quads; far facades that keep every balcony bar (the residential tower far level 140,962 triangles, the large made-to-measure tower 157,990); MakeHuman shoes up to 153,938 triangles a pair, hair up to 120,022, strand-by-strand brows and lashes of 14,000-42,000 next to 192-triangle strips of the same piece, two eyes of 2,040, a pearl necklace of 56,064, weapons and instruments no city figure carries; the same garment loaded twice under two names; a flat lake disc of 4,624 and a flat far ground plate of 2,560.
  • Hidden triangles everywhere: 270,217 over the catalogue.
